How to Restore Your Lifetime Pro License on Any Windows 10/11 PC

✓ Verified Instant Solution
  1. Open the Microsoft Store app on Windows and verify you are signed in with the exact Microsoft Account used to purchase Pro Lifetime ($3.99).
  2. Open Quick Audio Switcher → Settings (Gear Icon) → Account & Pro Status and click Restore Purchase / Sync Store License.
  3. Your digital license is tied to your Microsoft Account permanently (supports up to 10 Windows devices with zero recurring subscriptions). If Microsoft Store cache is stale, run wsreset.exe from the Start Menu and restart the app.
